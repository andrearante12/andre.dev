One camera, two kinds of truth.

There is no lidar. A single depth camera in each robot's head has to both map the warehouse and notice what is moving in front of it — so the image is split into two versions of reality, each sent to the consumer that wants it. The map keeps the building; the navigator keeps the traffic.

The sensor suite

Each robot fuses a forward RGB-D camera with inertial and odometric motion priors. RTAB-Map runs with visual odometry off — it consumes the wheel/odometry odom → base_link transform as its motion model and waits for the first IMU sample before it starts.

SensorPartRole
RGB-D cameraIntel RealSense D435depth + colour for SLAM and the Nav2 costmaps
IMUMPU-6050 classorientation prior; RTAB-Map blocks on the first sample

Wheel encodersdrive baseodom and the odom → base_link TF

TF frames are prefixed per robot (robot_0/base_link, …) on the shared /tf. Autonomy must never look up another robot's frame there — that would be a pose oracle. The coordination radio is the only channel for peer state.

The dynamic-obstacle filter

dynamic_obstacle_filter detects movers by frame-to-frame depth change, closes speckle with a morphological pass, and keeps robot-sized blobs. It is vision-only — no peer TF. Mover pixels are written to NaN in a re-encoded 32FC1 image so they are never painted into SLAM; the same blobs are published separately as an ephemeral point cloud for a Nav2 obstacle layer that clears the instant they leave the frustum.

Occupancy-grid tuning

Frontier exploration is literally "a free cell adjacent to an unknown cell" — with no believable free space there is nothing to explore toward. Ray tracing is the load-bearing setting: without it the only cells marked free are ones where a depth point happened to land on the floor, and /map stays overwhelmingly unknown even in rooms a robot drove straight through.

ParameterValueWhy
Grid/RayTracingtruemarks free space the camera swept, not just where a point landed
Grid/3Dtruemakes cloud_map real 3D geometry, not a 15 cm pancake — the dashboard's 3D panel depends on it
Grid/RangeMax5.0 mmatches the costmap raytrace range; bounds the per-node volume cost of 3D
GridGlobal/ProbHit / ProbMiss0.8 / 0.45reweighted — 3D ray tracing floods far more free evidence into the projected 2D grid than the defaults expect

Measured on the primitive scene after ~80 s of driving: 52.9 m² free, 54.8% of the grid, versus a near-empty map before the tuning.

Field notes

Diagnosed live · 2026-08-09

The blob labeller that blinded both costmaps

Connected-component labelling started as a per-pixel flood fill: at 848×480 that is ~407,000 interpreted iterations per frame, which throttled depth_static to about 0.3 Hz. The knock-on was the expensive part — depth_image_proc keeps a camera_info queue only five messages deep, so it never synced an image/info pair, published no point cloud, and left both costmaps and the collision monitor with no obstacle data. Rewritten as row-runs plus union-find, with blob sizes from one bincount over run lengths.

Diagnosed live · 2026-08-01

Free space floods the map when ray tracing is on

Turning on 3D ray tracing pushed far more "this cell is empty" evidence into the projected 2D grid than the hit/miss probabilities were tuned for. Reweighting ProbHit/ProbMiss from 0.7/0.4 to 0.8/0.45 took occupied cells from 7,361 down to 659 while free space roughly doubled.
